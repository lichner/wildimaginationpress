It's finally here, and I am so thrilled to share it with you!

**One Puppy, Two Puppies, Hey What Do You Do?** is now available as a paperback on Amazon, and I honestly can't stop smiling about it. This little book has been such a joy to create, and I hope it brings just as much joy into your home as it has into mine.

## What Is It All About?

At its heart it is a counting book. We count from one to ten alongside the most adorable, mischievous puppies you've ever seen: running through sunshine, digging in the garden, splashing in puddles, tugging on a rope, jumping for treats. And at every turn, the book invites your little one to join in: *"Come do it too!"*

The rhyming text bounces along in a rhythm toddlers naturally love, and I built the repetition in on purpose. It is the kind of book that gets read again and again (and again!), which happens to be exactly how little ones learn best. When the learning feels like play, they never notice it happening.

## What Makes This Book Special

The numbers themselves are characters. Each number from 1 to 10 appears throughout the book as a little personality of its own, waving, dancing, cheering the puppies on. Children spot them, point at them, and start recognising numbers as friendly faces rather than abstract squiggles.

I also filled the pages with as many breeds as I could fit: golden retrievers, dalmatians, beagles, pugs, poodles, dachshunds, corgis and more. Little dog lovers get a whole second game out of spotting and naming them all.

And then there is the ending. After all that running and jumping and splashing, everything winds down to the most tender moment: a child cuddled up with their one special puppy at bedtime. *"But I love just one, my one and only pup, to cuddle before sleep... we snuggle right up!"* I won't lie, it gets me every time.

## Who Is This Book For?

I wrote it for toddlers and preschoolers aged 0 to 4, though really for any household where somebody is mad about puppies. It suits parents after a bedtime book that teaches counting without feeling like a lesson, educators who want a read-aloud that gets a whole room of children moving, and grandparents hunting for the right present for a dog-obsessed little one.

The interactive prompts make it wonderful for group reading too. Picture a room full of toddlers pretending to splash in puddles or jump for treats, and you have the idea.
